CROP: Redgram
TELUGU NAME: కందులు
ALTERNATE NAME: Pigeonpea / Tur
CROP TYPE: Pulse / Legume crop

GENERAL INFORMATION:
Redgram is an important pulse crop.
It is grown under different rainfall and soil conditions.
Crop performance depends on variety, sowing time,
soil moisture and pest management.

COMMON CROP PROBLEMS:
1. Fusarium wilt: plants may yellow and wilt.
2. Sterility mosaic: plants may show excessive branching
   and reduced flowering.
3. Pod borer: caterpillars may damage flowers and pods.
4. Pod fly: larvae may damage developing seeds.
5. Waterlogging: roots may be affected and plants may wilt.

SAFE PRECAUTIONS:
- Inspect leaves, flowers and pods regularly.
- Check for caterpillars and pod damage.
- Maintain proper field drainage.
- Monitor plants for unusual yellowing and wilting.
- Remove severely affected plant material appropriately.
- Avoid unnecessary pesticide applications.

INFORMATION TO COLLECT:
- Crop age and variety.
- Rainfed or irrigated crop.
- Whether flowering or pod formation has started.
- Presence of caterpillars or damaged pods.
- Whether plants are yellowing or wilting.
- Whether symptoms are spreading across the field.

FERTILIZER SAFETY:
Use soil-test-based recommendations matching
the local soil and crop stage.

CROP CALENDAR - REDGRAM:

Redgram (Pigeonpea) is mainly cultivated
as a Kharif rainfed crop in Andhra Pradesh.
It can also be cultivated during Rabi
in suitable regions with irrigation.

1. KHARIF SOWING:

General reference period:
June 15 to August 15.

Regional guidance:
- Low rainfall zones: June.
- Coastal Andhra zones: July to August.
- YSR Kadapa research: July first or second
  fortnight was suitable for tested long-
  and medium-duration varieties under
  the studied rainfed conditions.

2. RABI SOWING:

General reference period:
September 20 to October 20.

Rabi cultivation is suitable only in
appropriate regions and farming conditions.
Irrigation availability and variety selection
are important.

IMPORTANT:
- Redgram is generally directly sown using seeds.
- Do not confuse direct sowing with nursery planting.
- Sowing dates depend on district, rainfall,
  soil moisture, variety and crop duration.
- Do not present research results from one district
  as recommendations for every district.
- Do not assume Rabi cultivation is suitable
  for every rainfed field.

Ask the farmer:
- Which district?
- Is the crop rainfed or irrigated?
- Which variety are you planning to grow?
- Are you planning Kharif or Rabi cultivation?

Do not invent fixed dates or fertilizer doses.
