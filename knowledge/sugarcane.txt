CROP: Sugarcane
TELUGU NAME: చెరకు
CROP TYPE: Commercial / Industrial crop

GENERAL INFORMATION:
Sugarcane is a long-duration commercial crop.
Growth depends on variety, planting material,
soil fertility, irrigation and crop management.

COMMON CROP PROBLEMS:
1. Red rot: internal stem tissue may show red discoloration.
2. Smut: a black whip-like structure may develop.
3. Early shoot borer: young shoots may dry.
4. Internode borer: holes and damage may appear
   around sugarcane internodes.
5. Water stress: leaf rolling and poor growth may occur.

SAFE PRECAUTIONS:
- Use healthy planting material.
- Inspect young shoots and stems regularly.
- Check for holes and unusual drying.
- Maintain proper field drainage.
- Remove diseased plants and residues according
  to local agricultural guidance.
- Do not use affected planting material for a new crop.

INFORMATION TO COLLECT:
- Variety and crop age.
- Plant crop or ratoon crop.
- Whether young shoots or mature stems are affected.
- Presence of holes, black structures or red discoloration.
- Irrigation and drainage conditions.

FERTILIZER SAFETY:
Recommendations depend on soil test,
variety, crop duration and local recommendations.

CROP CALENDAR - SUGARCANE:

Sugarcane has different planting periods depending
on the agro-climatic region and variety.

1. COASTAL ANDHRA PRADESH:
- General planting period: October to November.
- Another planting period: second half of December
  to the end of February.

2. INTERIOR ANDHRA PRADESH:
- General planting period: January to February.

3. SOUTHERN ANDHRA PRADESH:
An ANGRAU-associated research study in Chittoor
district found:
- January planting suitable for mid-late varieties.
- February planting suitable for early varieties.

These are research-based reference periods.
Do not treat them as fixed recommendations
for every district or variety.

PLANT CROP AND RATOON CROP:

Plant crop:
The first sugarcane crop established using
healthy sugarcane setts.

Ratoon crop:
The crop that grows again from the stubble
after harvesting the previous sugarcane crop.

Ratoon management and timing depend on
the previous crop's harvest date, variety,
crop health and local conditions.

IMPORTANT:
- Do not confuse plant crop with ratoon crop.
- Do not give the plant crop planting calendar
  as ratoon crop advice.
- Consider district, variety, soil, irrigation,
  rainfall and planting season.
- Use healthy planting material.
- Do not invent fertilizer doses or treatments.

Ask the farmer:
- Which district?
- Which sugarcane variety?
- Is this a new plant crop or a ratoon crop?
- If ratoon, when was the previous crop harvested?
