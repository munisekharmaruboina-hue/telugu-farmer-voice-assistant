CROP: Banana
TELUGU NAME: అరటి
CROP TYPE: Fruit crop

GENERAL INFORMATION:
Banana is an important fruit crop.
Growth depends on variety, soil, water availability,
planting material and nutrient management.

COMMON CROP PROBLEMS:
1. Sigatoka leaf spot: spots and drying may appear on leaves.
2. Fusarium wilt: yellowing and wilting may occur.
3. Bunchy top: leaves may become narrow and crowded
   near the top of the plant.
4. Root problems: poor growth and plant instability may occur.
5. Fruit damage: fruits may show abnormal marks or rotting.

SAFE PRECAUTIONS:
- Use healthy planting material from reliable sources.
- Inspect leaves and pseudostems regularly.
- Check drainage and avoid prolonged water stagnation.
- Remove dead leaves when appropriate.
- Do not move suspected infected planting material
  to other fields.
- Avoid applying chemicals without identifying the cause.

INFORMATION TO COLLECT:
- Variety.
- Plant age.
- Whether the plant is in vegetative or bunch stage.
- Leaf spots, yellowing or bunchy growth.
- Whether one plant or many plants are affected.
- Irrigation and drainage conditions.

FERTILIZER SAFETY:
Nutrient recommendations depend on variety,
plant age, soil test and local horticultural guidance.

CROP CALENDAR - BANANA:

Banana is a perennial fruit crop.
Planting time depends on variety, planting material,
irrigation, rainfall and local climate.

ANDHRA PRADESH REFERENCE:

The Andhra Pradesh Farmer's Handbook on Basic
Agriculture gives 15 June to 15 July as an
optimum banana planting period.

Other agricultural references mention May planting
or May-June and September-October periods.
These periods are not confirmed as suitable
for every district in Andhra Pradesh.

IMPORTANT:
- Avoid planting during heavy rainfall and
  waterlogged field conditions.
- Ensure good field drainage.
- Tissue culture plants and sucker planting
  may have different planting requirements.
- Do not treat one calendar period as suitable
  for every variety and district.

Ask the farmer:
- Which district?
- Which banana variety?
- Are they using tissue culture plants or suckers?
- Is the field rainfed or irrigated?

Do not give a fixed planting date without
considering local conditions.
