CROP: Paddy
TELUGU NAME: వరి
CROP TYPE: Cereal / Food crop

GENERAL INFORMATION:
Paddy is an important food crop in Andhra Pradesh.
Crop growth depends on variety, season, soil,
water availability and crop management.

COMMON CROP PROBLEMS:
1. Leaf blast: spindle-shaped spots may appear on leaves.
2. Sheath blight: lesions may develop near the leaf sheath.
3. Bacterial leaf blight: leaves may show yellowing and drying.
4. Gall midge: abnormal swelling may appear in shoots.
5. Water stress: poor water availability can affect crop growth.

SAFE PRECAUTIONS:
- Inspect the field regularly.
- Observe leaves, stems, roots and panicles.
- Avoid excessive or insufficient irrigation.
- Keep field bunds and drainage channels in good condition.
- Do not apply fertilizer or pesticide without identifying the problem.
- Follow locally recommended crop-management practices.

INFORMATION TO COLLECT:
- Crop age and variety.
- Kharif or Rabi season.
- Transplanted or direct-seeded crop.
- Irrigation and recent rainfall.
- Which plant parts show symptoms?
- Is the problem spreading across the field?

FERTILIZER SAFETY:
Fertilizer recommendations depend on soil test,
season, variety and crop growth stage.
Do not generate a fertilizer dose without a matching
verified Andhra Pradesh recommendation.

CROP CALENDAR - ANDHRA PRADESH:

Paddy is cultivated during both Kharif and Rabi
seasons in Andhra Pradesh.

KHARIF:
Paddy cultivation generally follows the southwest
monsoon season.

RABI:
Paddy cultivation is also practiced in areas where
irrigation water is available.

HISTORICAL DISTRICT REFERENCE:
An ANGRAU/ICAR-CRIDA contingency plan for the
former Vizianagaram district (2011) gives these
reference sowing windows:

- Kharif rainfed: June first fortnight to August
  first fortnight.
- Kharif irrigated: June first fortnight to July
  second fortnight.
- Rabi irrigated: November second fortnight to
  January first fortnight.

These historical district-specific dates must not
be treated as current statewide recommendations.

Sowing and nursery timing may differ from the
transplanting date.

Suitable timing depends on:
- Current district and agro-climatic zone.
- Kharif or Rabi season.
- Direct sowing or transplanting.
- Irrigation availability.
- Rainfall and water availability.
- Variety and crop duration.

Do not give a fixed sowing or transplanting date
without sufficient local information.

Ask the farmer for their district, season and
whether the crop is direct-seeded or transplanted.
