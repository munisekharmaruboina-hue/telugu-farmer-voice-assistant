
CROP: Tomato
TELUGU NAME: టమాట
CROP TYPE: Vegetable crop

GENERAL INFORMATION:
Tomato is an important vegetable crop.
It requires suitable soil, sunlight, irrigation
and good field drainage.

COMMON PESTS:
1. Whitefly (తెల్లదోమ): Small white insects may be
   visible on the undersides of leaves. They may fly
   when the plant is disturbed. They can contribute
   to the spread of tomato leaf curl viruses.
2. Aphids (పేనుబంక): Small soft-bodied insects may
   appear in groups on tender shoots and leaves.
3. Thrips (తామర పురుగులు): Tiny insects may cause
   silvery patches and leaf distortion.
4. Fruit borer (కాయ తొలుచు పురుగు): Caterpillars may
   damage tomato fruits.

COMMON DISEASES AND PROBLEMS:
1. Early blight: Brown spots with circular rings.
2. Septoria leaf spot: Small spots with grey centres
   and dark margins.
3. Bacterial leaf spot: Water-soaked spots may develop.
4. Fusarium wilt: Plants may yellow and wilt.
5. Leaf curl: Leaves may curl and plants may become
   stunted. Viral infection is one possible cause.
6. Root-knot nematodes: Roots may develop abnormal knots.

SAFE PRECAUTIONS:
- Inspect old and new leaves separately.
- Check the undersides of leaves for insects.
- Observe whether white insects fly when plants are disturbed.
- Check whether spots have rings or water-soaked edges.
- Check whether plants are wilting despite moist soil.
- Avoid water stagnation.
- Monitor the spread of the problem.
- Avoid unnecessary fertilizer and pesticide applications.
- Do not diagnose a disease from one symptom alone.
- Do not recommend chemical treatment without reliable
  identification and locally verified guidance.

INFORMATION TO COLLECT:
- Crop age and variety.
- Whether old or new leaves are affected.
- Presence of spots, curling or wilting.
- Whether white insects or other pests are visible.
- Number of affected plants.
- Whether the problem is spreading.
- Irrigation and recent weather.

FERTILIZER SAFETY:
Recommendations depend on soil test,
crop stage and local agricultural guidance.

PESTICIDE SAFETY:
Do not invent pesticide names, quantities or spray schedules.
Use only verified recommendations for the identified pest
and crop stage from authorized local agricultural sources.

CROP CALENDAR - TOMATO:

Tomato is generally cultivated during Kharif,
Rabi and Summer seasons.

NURSERY RAISING - ANDHRA PRADESH REFERENCE:

Kharif: June to July.
Rabi: October to November.
Summer: January to February.

TRANSPLANTING:

Nursery sowing and main-field transplanting
are two different operations.

Healthy tomato seedlings are generally ready
for transplanting around 25-30 days after sowing,
depending on variety and seedling growth.

Do not describe nursery sowing months as
main-field transplanting months.

For transplanting advice, first consider:
- Date of nursery sowing.
- Current age and growth of seedlings.
- District and local weather.
- Rainfed or irrigated cultivation.

If the nursery sowing date is unknown,
ask the farmer when the seedlings were sown.
Do not invent a fixed transplanting date.

IMPORTANT:
Nursery sowing and main-field transplanting
are different operations.

These are general reference periods, not
guaranteed dates for every Andhra Pradesh district.

Suitable timing depends on:
- District and local climate.
- Season and rainfall.
- Rainfed or irrigated cultivation.
- Soil type and drainage.
- Variety and seedling growth.

Ask the farmer for their district and whether
they mean nursery sowing or transplanting.

Do not give a fixed date without sufficient
local information.
