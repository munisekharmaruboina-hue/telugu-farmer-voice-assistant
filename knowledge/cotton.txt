CROP: Cotton
TELUGU NAME: పత్తి
CROP TYPE: Commercial / Fibre crop

GENERAL INFORMATION:
Cotton is an important commercial crop in Andhra Pradesh.
Its growth depends on variety, soil, rainfall,
irrigation and pest management.

COMMON CROP PROBLEMS:
1. Pink bollworm: larvae damage developing cotton bolls.
2. Whitefly: small white insects may be seen under leaves.
3. Aphids: small insects may cluster on tender plant parts.
4. Jassids: leaf edges may turn yellow or reddish.
5. Leaf spots and wilt: fungal or bacterial problems
   may cause spots, yellowing or plant drying.

SAFE PRECAUTIONS:
- Inspect flowers, squares and cotton bolls regularly.
- Check the underside of leaves for sucking pests.
- Monitor pheromone traps where recommended.
- Remove crop residues according to local guidance.
- Follow crop rotation and integrated pest management.
- Do not spray pesticides just because insects are visible.

INFORMATION TO COLLECT:
- Crop age and variety.
- Rainfed or irrigated crop.
- Whether insects are present on leaves or inside bolls.
- Whether flowers or bolls are damaged.
- Number of affected plants and field distribution.

FERTILIZER SAFETY:
Use recommendations matching the cotton type,
soil test and local agricultural advisory.

SOURCES:
ICAR - Kharif Agro-Advisories 2025.
ICAR - Andhra Pradesh agricultural information.


CROP CALENDAR - ANDHRA PRADESH:

Cotton is mainly cultivated during the Kharif season.

REFERENCE SOWING PERIODS:

For red soils:
June to the first fortnight of July.

For black soils:
July to the first fortnight of August.

These periods are based on the ICAR-CICR
Package of Practices for Cotton Cultivation
in Andhra Pradesh, 2023-2024.

Actual sowing time depends on:
- District and local rainfall.
- Soil type.
- Rainfed or irrigated cultivation.
- Monsoon onset and soil moisture.

Do not treat these periods as guaranteed dates
for every location or cotton variety.

Ask the farmer for their district, soil type
and whether cultivation is rainfed or irrigated.
