CROP: Mango
TELUGU NAME: మామిడి
CROP TYPE: Fruit / Orchard crop

GENERAL INFORMATION:
Mango is an important fruit crop in Andhra Pradesh.
Orchard management depends on variety, tree age,
soil, rainfall, irrigation and flowering season.

COMMON CROP PROBLEMS:
1. Powdery mildew: white powder-like growth may appear
   on flowers or young plant parts.
2. Anthracnose: dark spots may appear on leaves,
   flowers or fruits.
3. Mango hoppers: small insects may be seen around
   flower panicles and tender shoots.
4. Malformation: abnormal bunchy growth may occur
   in shoots or flower panicles.
5. Fruit drop: young fruits may fall for several reasons.

SAFE PRECAUTIONS:
- Inspect new shoots, leaves and flower panicles.
- Monitor fruit development and fruit drop.
- Remove fallen and diseased plant material appropriately.
- Maintain orchard cleanliness.
- Avoid unnecessary irrigation changes and chemical sprays.
- Do not assume all fruit drop is caused by insects.

INFORMATION TO COLLECT:
- Tree age and variety.
- Flowering, fruit-setting or fruit-development stage.
- Whether symptoms affect leaves, flowers or fruits.
- Presence of insects.
- Whether many trees or only a few are affected.
- Recent rainfall and irrigation.

FERTILIZER SAFETY:
Recommendations depend on tree age, soil test,
variety and local horticultural guidance.

CROP CALENDAR - MANGO:

Mango is a perennial orchard crop.
Planting, flowering and harvesting are three
different stages and must not be confused.

1. ORCHARD PLANTING - ANDHRA PRADESH:

The general recommended planting period is
June to July, at the beginning of the monsoon.

In areas with heavy rainfall, planting may be
taken up towards the end of the rainy season.

Use healthy, grafted planting material from
a reliable nursery.

2. FLOWERING SEASON:

Mango flowering in Andhra Pradesh generally
occurs around December to February.
The exact period varies with variety,
local climate and orchard conditions.

3. HARVESTING SEASON:

Mango harvesting in Andhra Pradesh generally
takes place from March to June.
The period varies by variety and location.

IMPORTANT:
- Do not confuse orchard planting with flowering.
- Do not confuse flowering with fruit harvesting.
- These are general reference periods, not
  guaranteed dates for every district.
- Consider variety, district, rainfall,
  irrigation and local climate.
- Do not recommend withholding irrigation
  or using chemicals merely to induce flowering.

Ask the farmer whether they mean:
orchard planting, flowering or fruit harvesting.
