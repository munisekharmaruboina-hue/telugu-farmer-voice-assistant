CROP: Chilli
TELUGU NAME: మిరప
CROP TYPE: Spice / Horticultural crop

GENERAL INFORMATION:
Chilli is an important commercial horticultural crop.
Crop health depends on variety, soil, irrigation,
weather and pest management.

COMMON CROP PROBLEMS:
1. Thrips: leaf curling, scarring or silvery patches may occur.
2. Mites: leaves may become curled or distorted.
3. Leaf curl virus: leaves may curl and plants may become stunted.
4. Anthracnose: dark sunken spots may appear on fruits.
5. Fusarium wilt: plants may yellow and wilt.
6. Fruit rot: affected fruits may become soft or discoloured.

SAFE PRECAUTIONS:
- Inspect young leaves and the underside of leaves.
- Monitor flowers and fruits for damage.
- Avoid excessive irrigation and water stagnation.
- Remove fallen and severely diseased plant material safely.
- Maintain suitable spacing and field sanitation.
- Do not assume leaf curling always means a virus.

INFORMATION TO COLLECT:
- Crop age and variety.
- Nursery or main-field stage.
- Whether leaves are curled, spotted or yellow.
- Whether insects are visible.
- Whether fruits have dark spots or rotting.
- Recent weather and irrigation.

FERTILIZER SAFETY:
Use soil-test-based recommendations suitable
for the local region and crop stage.

CROP CALENDAR - CHILLI:

Chilli can be cultivated under both rainfed
and irrigated conditions.

GENERAL REFERENCE PERIODS:

KHARIF NURSERY SOWING:
General reference period: May to June.

MAIN-FIELD TRANSPLANTING:
General reference period: July to August.

Do not describe the transplanting period
as the nursery sowing period.

IRRIGATED CROPS:
The advisory also mentions October or
January-February sowing periods for irrigated crops.

IMPORTANT:
These are general ICAR reference periods.
They are not confirmed district-specific
recommendations for every part of Andhra Pradesh.

Nursery sowing and main-field transplanting
are different operations.

Suitable timing depends on:
- District and local climate.
- Rainfed or irrigated cultivation.
- Soil type and drainage.
- Rainfall and soil moisture.
- Chilli variety and crop purpose.

Do not give a fixed date without sufficient
local information.

Ask the farmer for their district,
cultivation method and whether they mean
nursery sowing or main-field transplanting.
